Cutview - Comic Reader Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 9, 2026

Cutview is a local viewer for image folders and ZIP/CBZ files you select. It does not require an account and does not use analytics servers. On iOS and Android, the free version shows ads from Google (see Advertising). The app does not send your comic files or reading history to a developer server.

Advertising
On iOS and Android, Cutview shows a banner at the bottom of the library and book list. Ads never appear while you are reading. The Windows and macOS versions have no ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, and may use cookies or similar identifiers. Cutview requests non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps. Your comic files, file names, library and reading history are not included in ad requests.

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your choice before showing ads. You can change it later in Settings > About > Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, the app keeps working without ads where the law requires that.

Google's processing is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Removing ads
You can remove ads with a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. The app stores only whether ads are removed, on your device, and sends no purchase information to a developer server. If you bought Cutview while it was a paid app on the App Store, you never see ads. To check this, the app reads the original purchase date from the App Store on your device and keeps the result on your device.

Information stored on your device
The app stores your library list, file access permissions, reading positions, per-book display adjustments, app settings and thumbnail cache on your device. Diagnostic logs used to investigate errors also remain on the device. To avoid frequent review requests, it stores the installation date, internal identifiers of completed books, value-action count, review API request count and request dates locally. An incoming app link may remain on your device until it can be opened safely. It is not sent to a developer server. Original comic files are read only and are not modified.

Deleting information
You can clear the cache and reading history in Settings > Data. Removing a library source deletes its library entry and associated reading history and per-book settings. Thumbnail caches can be cleared separately. Local diagnostic logs are not immediately deleted when a source is removed. Review request history and pending links remain with the app data. Clearing the app data in operating system settings or removing the app deletes them according to operating system behavior. Deleting app data does not delete original comic files.

Operating system and external services
Cloud files that you select in the system file picker may be handled by the operating system or the file provider. Apple and Google purchases, store reviews, TestFlight, system diagnostics and operating system backups are subject to the relevant provider's policies and your settings. Selecting Rate this app or another app opens an external store application. The other-app catalog and icons are bundled with Cutview and are not downloaded at runtime.

Information you choose to share
If you email us for support, we use the information and reply address you provide to respond to your request. Screenshots or diagnostic files you choose to send may include filenames. Please review them before sharing. The app does not send this information automatically.
You can request deletion of support materials at the contact address below. Unless retention is required by law, materials that are no longer needed to handle your request are deleted.
